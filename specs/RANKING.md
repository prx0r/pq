## What this means for qp-goose

No single zip is the system. The 9s supply the verifier discipline and the
reference loop, the 8s supply the executable runtime and formalization, the
7s supply allocation doctrine, and the originals supply the frozen core
(`/home/ubuntu/qp`) plus the constitution (`/agentcomfinal`). The plan in
`docs/QP_GOOSE_PLAN.md` crowns one lineage per layer and names the five
integration seams as the actual build.
